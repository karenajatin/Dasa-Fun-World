Spend pearls you collect on dives. Upgrades are kept forever.

How to play

  • Steer: touch and drag anywhere on the left side. Matsya moves the way you drag.
  • Collect the glowing orange black-box fragments ◆, then reach the exit beacon. The small arrow shows the way.
  • Oxygen runs down. Hover over bubble vents to refill.
  • Hull: fast bumps, jellyfish, eels, anglerfish and hot plumes cause damage. Slow and steady is safe.
  • 💡 Light lets you see in the dark but uses battery, and anglerfish follow light. ◉ Sonar shows the cave walls for a moment.
  • Checkpoints (green buoys) save your progress inside a dive.
  • Pearls buy upgrades: stronger hull, bigger oxygen tank, brighter lamp.
  • Stars: ★ finish · ★★ all fragments · ★★★ hull 50%+ and under par time.
  • Keyboard: arrows / WASD to steer, Space = sonar, L = light, P = pause.
